Client Policies

Payment Policy

▼

Standard Payment Structure: 70% deposit before work begins | 30% before final delivery

For smaller projects: 100% upfront may apply

Revision Policy

▶

Each package includes a limited number of revisions.

Revision includes: Minor adjustments, color changes, font adjustments, layout refinements, small modifications.

Revision does NOT include: Complete redesign, new creative direction, new concept development, additional deliverables outside agreed scope.

Additional revisions beyond package limits: $10 per revision

Approval Policy

▶

Once a design concept is approved, major changes become billable. New concepts require additional payment. Timeline extensions may apply.

Communication Policy

▶

Official channels: Email, WhatsApp Business, Scheduled meetings

Business hours: Monday – Saturday | 8:00 AM – 7:00 PM

Refund Policy

▶

Deposits are non-refundable once work has commenced.

Refunds will not apply for: Change of mind, delayed responses from client, scope changes, approved concepts.

File Delivery Policy

▶

Final files are delivered after full payment.

Deliverables may include: PNG, JPG, PDF, SVG, Source files (if included in package).

Turnaround Policy

▶

Project timelines depend on scope, complexity, client responsiveness, and revision rounds.

Rush projects attract additional charges.
